## Task
[CASMI 2026](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/enveda-CASMI26-molecule-id-mass-spectra): rank
up to 25 structures per molecule from its LC-MS/MS spectra (400 molecules, 1213 spectra). Metric:
MRR@25 on the InChIKey connectivity block. Notebook-only, internet off; only the public split is
visible.

## Approach
The current best pipeline,
[`kaggle_submission.py`](https://github.com/charleneleong-ai/ai4science/blob/feat/casmi-kaggle-notebook/casmi/notebooks/kaggle_submission.py):
1000 best-documented PubChem connectivities in a 5 ppm window plus train structures, minus the train
structure behind each copied spectrum; ranked by an FPNet ensemble (f·z) plus 0.25 × popularity;
same-formula groups in the top 60 re-ordered by z(score) + 1.0 z(ICEBERG) + 1.0 z(GLACIER). Inputs
and checkpoints with versions:
[experiment card](https://github.com/charleneleong-ai/ai4science/blob/feat/casmi-kaggle-notebook/casmi/docs/experiment-card.md);
summary:
[report](https://github.com/charleneleong-ai/ai4science/blob/feat/casmi-kaggle-notebook/casmi/docs/report.pdf).
