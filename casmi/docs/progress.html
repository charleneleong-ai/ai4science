What is not established

  • No local estimate of the score. Train-derived labels were wrong, and consensus labels from ten public notebooks failed calibration: three lineages that agree on 12 of 400 top-1 picks.
  • The private score. The shortlist size, the forward-model weight and GLACIER's input and v19's similarity threshold were chosen on the public split, so 0.356 is optimistic.
  • Candidate recall on the test set. On held-out train molecules the popularity shortlist holds the answer for 126 / 280, and a spectrum-driven second pass for 236 / 280 (MRR@25 0.241 → 0.405). On the test set the same pass scored 0.353 against 0.349: the production FPNet memorised the copied spectra and steered the pass toward the copied structures (median Tanimoto of changed top-1 picks 0.26 → 0.42). Skipping candidates with Tanimoto ≥ 0.5 to the copied structure scored 0.356 (v19). How much test recall the pass buys is unmeasured.
  • Why the data looks like this. 1189 of 1213 test spectra are bit-identical to a train row, yet that row's structure is the answer for at most about 46 of 400 molecules.
  • 29 molecules get no forward-model evidence.
