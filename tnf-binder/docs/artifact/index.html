Adaptyv × Anthropic · Challenge 02 · TNF-α

The verifier is the asset

Four generative pipelines produced candidate binders to TNF-α. They were close to interchangeable. What decided the outcome was the stack of checks behind them — and the single highest-scoring design in the whole campaign turned out to be ubiquitin.

57 constructs in, 3 out

Each gate below ran on designs that had already passed everything above it. The attrition is the result; the three survivors alone would be marketing.

57 Constructs entering the gates BoltzGen parents and engineered histidine variants

What each verifier caught

Composition A sequence that is 59% alanine scored 0.556 binder-to-target ipTM and ranked first in a 40-design shortlist. Confidence models score degenerate sequences confidently, so this check deliberately uses no model at all.
Seq novelty The top-ranked construct of the campaign — best interface score, lowest interface PDE at 0.89 Å, carrying an ideal-pKa histidine — was verbatim human ubiquitin. Every metric said submit it first.
Interface Contact-pair counting was implemented first and was actively misleading: counts scale with interpenetration, so clashing poly-alanine out-scored real designs. Replaced with buried surface area.
Fold novelty Predicted the one real-world novelty failure: a β-sandwich matching CATH 2.60.40 at TM-score 0.893 over 88.8% coverage.
pKa switch Histidines placed by first-principles reasoning were all dead — predicted pKa 3.15, 4.70 and 5.71 against a window of 6.0–7.4.

Fold, not sequence, is the constraint

One design was submitted and failed the organiser's automatic novelty check; two passed. They differ by 0.6 percentage points of sequence identity, so sequence novelty cannot explain the split.

Correction

This gate was first reported as reproducing all three verdicts. That was wrong: the two passes scored correctly only because a missing sequence-identity value defaulted to zero. With the real 38.1% it returns 2 where the organiser returned ≥3. It is a conservative pre-filter — decisive on failure, advisory on borderline.

Eight hypotheses, eight refutations

Everything reasoned from first principles was overturned by measurement. Everything that survived came from testing against a control.

HypothesisWhat the data said

Carboxylate proximity raises pKa into the window r = +0.21; a site with net +6 carboxylates sits at pKa 3.6

A concavity heuristic picks a better epitope Null: 0.198 for both the new pocket and the original lobe
Truncating the trimer to a dimer is harmless Cost 0.11 interface confidence — more than any deliberate design choice gained

No single score is truth

The predictors disagree on identical structures, so interface confidence is reported as a range with the models named.

Self-confirmation

AlphaFold2 rated its own gradient-optimised designs at i_pTM 0.92. An independent predictor rated the same structures 0.21.

Global ipTM is unusable here

The TNF trimer's own internal interfaces score ~0.88 and dominate the mean. Swapping dimer for trimer lifted global ipTM 0.20 → 0.52 while the binder interface stayed flat.

Best-of-n inflates

Metrics are stored per diffusion sample. n attempts give the oracle n chances to pass a design, so ranking uses the mean.

The epitope, computed

TNF-α residues within 4.5 Å of TNFR2 in 3ALQ, intersected with a human/mouse alignment (124/157 = 79.0%, reproducing the stated figure): 33 contact positions, 24 conserved and 9 divergent.

R31→Q is the trap

It carries the largest solvent-accessible surface in the entire epitope — 193 Å² — and sits beside R32 in the 29–33 loop that adalimumab and infliximab engage. That is why neither drug is mouse cross-reactive. Any design gripping that loop wins objective 1 and forfeits objective 2.

What is not established

No expression, no size-exclusion chromatography, no thermal stability, no SPR or BLI. There is no measured binding of any kind. pH selectivity rests on predicted pKa — no folding model represents protonation, and the assay is not simulated. Mouse cross-reactivity rests on a predicted structure and conserved-epitope coverage. The three refuted pH mechanisms have no validated replacement; switch positions come from an exhaustive scan of 200 substitutions, which found 18 ideal and 17 usable.

A generated sequence that passes computational checks is a candidate. A result is a measured biological endpoint. This work produced the former.
